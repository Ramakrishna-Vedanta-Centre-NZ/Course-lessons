Practice · Lesson 5.4

Action and Offering

The Mind in the Light of Modern and Spiritual Sciences · Advanced

Lesson 5.4 takes practice out of the sitting and into the other fifteen or sixteen hours of the day. Here you will live one day, see how a loop of habit forms and where it can be reached, tell real non-attached action from its look-alikes, hold the reins of a carriage, and lower the noise in a signal.

Parts III and V are scored: your first pick counts. Parts I, II and IV are practice and are not scored.

Part I

A day in the filter

Practice: not scored

The lesson says the sitting is a small fraction of waking life and that every minute of the rest is writing into h(t). (§1) Live one made-up day. The line shows the weight of the filter h(t), as a simple model. Start with the sitting, then meet six ordinary moments and choose how to meet each.

Sunrise

The line is an illustration of the lesson’s idea, not a measurement of anyone’s mind. These moments are made up. If even starting anything feels impossible, the lesson says the rhythm-and-sound family of Lesson 5.2 must do its work first. (§5)

Part IV

The coachman and the offering

Practice: not scored

Swami Vivekananda asks: A carriage with four horses may rush down a hill unrestrained or the coachman may curb the horses. Which is the greater manifestation of power, to let them go or to hold them? (§2.4) Try both.

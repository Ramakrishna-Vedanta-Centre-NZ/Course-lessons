    Scanning is not the problem

    This scanning function is not a defect. It is biologically necessary. But there is a difference between responsive flexibility and compulsive scattering. The first serves life. The second fragments it. (§1)

      Simple, and foundational

      In every case, the practice works by reducing the switching-load on manas. These practices are not preliminary exercises to be outgrown but foundational disciplines that support everything else. (§6)

      The training does not happen through suppression, and its starting point is often not some advanced mystical technique but the simplest of practices: sit still, breathe slowly, and give the mind one thing to rest on. (§7)

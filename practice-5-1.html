The Recipe and the Direction

Lesson 5.1 says what Module 5 is for, why now, and which way the practices point. Part I

Four ingredients, one dish

Scored: first pick counts

In his letter to Mr Sturdy, Swami Vivekananda says he wants to give dry, hard reason, softened in the sweetest syrup of love and made spicy with intense work, and cooked in the kitchen of Yoga. The lesson says the four ingredients are exact. For each ingredient, pick the Yoga and lesson the lesson pairs it with. Your first pick counts.

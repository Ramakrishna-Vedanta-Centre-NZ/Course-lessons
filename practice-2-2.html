The Inner Instrument at Work

The Mind in the Light of Modern and Spiritual Sciences · Advanced

The mind is not one thing. It is one inner instrument, the antaḥkaraṇa, with four functions: manas, buddhi, ahaṁkāra and chitta. Here you will follow a single ordinary moment through all four, then locate a difficulty in the function where it mainly belongs, and finally see the four as one.

Part III

One river

Practice: not scored

The four are not four organs sitting side by side. They are four modes of functioning within one inner instrument, like the way a single river can carry currents at different depths and speeds (§1).

Hold both truths

For the work of self-observation, the distinction between the Self and the instrument is indispensable: you need it to stop identifying with every passing disturbance. At a deeper level, the lesson adds, the distinction is a teaching tool and not the final truth. The instrument is not a second thing. It is the One Existence appearing in the form of mind, as waves are the ocean in motion (A Deeper View, after §2).

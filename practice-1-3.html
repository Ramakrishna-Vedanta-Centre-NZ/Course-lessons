Practice · Lesson 1.3

What Is Missing?

The Mind in the Light of Modern and Spiritual Sciences · Advanced

Lesson 1.3 says awareness is luminous and alive, and that the two cannot be separated. Here you will test that by prediction. First, for six things, decide what is absent and see how the lesson answers. Then compare two pictures of the mind: one that only records, and one that is living and self-shaping.

Part I is scored: your first check on each of the six cases counts. Part II is practice and is not scored.

Part I

What is missing?

Scored: first pick counts

Three things are in play: it reveals, it knows it reveals, and it is felt as presence. For each case below, mark what you think is absent. You may mark more than one, or choose that nothing is missing. Then check, and the lesson answers in its own words. Your first check on each case is the one that counts.

What the six show

  • A mirror, a screen and a camera each do something: reflect, display, record. Each lacks something the lesson names (§3).
  • Take away the knowing, and light that reveals is “a mechanical process”. Take away the revealing, and self-presence is “a closed point without content” (§4).
  • Awareness is both at once: “It illuminates, and it knows itself as illuminating” (§4). The light is Prakāśa, the living self-presence is Vimarśa, and the course names the one Reality Consciousness-Energy (§5).
  • They are not “two stages joined together” but “two aspects of one indivisible Reality”, as warmth and brightness are inseparable in a flame (§4).

This page illustrates an argument, and it speaks only of the lesson’s own examples. The discovery itself is in the two notices of §8: that whatever is present is being known, and that you are here.

Part II

Grooves

Practice: not scored

Lesson 1.3 sets two pictures of the mind side by side (§7). In the first, the mind is a recording device. In the second, it is a living, self-modifying system. Try the same simple actions in each and watch what changes, and what does not.

Which picture is this?

Someone says: “These patterns were deposited in me, and the task is to get them out.” Which picture of the mind is that, according to §7?

Hold it lightly

This is a picture of what the lesson claims, not a measurement. It has no timelines and says nothing about how long any change takes. The lesson also says its convergence with neuroscience “is not proof of the spiritual teaching”: contemplative inquiry from the inside and research from the outside illuminate each other “without reducing to each other” (§6).

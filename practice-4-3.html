What Can I Change, and How Fast?

The Mind in the Light of Modern and Spiritual Sciences · Advanced

Lesson 4.3 reads the wave equation as the framework in motion: each term has a role, something that sets it, and a pace at which it can change. Here you will tap through the terms, name each one from its role, and then watch two waves meet on a bench where only some dials change the beat.

You cannot change ψₓ by wishing. What you can change is everything that meets it on the inside: the filter h(t), the attention wave, and over time the noise floor n(t). (§3)

A filter is not the enemy

A filter is not, in itself, a problem. You need h(t). Without conditioning, you could not learn a language, recognise a friend, or play a musical instrument. (§3)

This is a drawing of the lesson’s mathematics. Moving a dial here is not a practice and says nothing about where anyone’s mind is. The object wave ψₓ is drawn stronger too, so the dial is only a picture: the lesson says you cannot change ψₓ by wishing (§3). Being slower or faster than fₛ is not better or worse: what the lesson asks is the match. The practices themselves are not taught on this page.

The drawing ends here: availability is not yet Self-Recognition, and that horizon lies beyond what the equation can describe. (§3)

Not a verdict on effort

The lesson’s point is structural: Each act of mental engagement that simply more strongly oscillates the existing duality is a doubling operation: it intensifies the loop without lifting out of it. (§3) The bench shows only that: both waves can grow while the beat stays.

Practice · Lesson 5.2

Rhythm and Sound

The Mind in the Light of Modern and Spiritual Sciences · Advanced

Lesson 5.2 says rhythm is the first and most direct practice, and that it works best when the rhythm is matched to the state of the mind. Here you will walk the map that shows why, breathe with a pacer, match a rhythm to two people, climb the ladder of sound, and sit with Holy Mother’s image of a candle in a still room.

Parts III and IV are scored: your first pick counts. Parts I, II and V are practice and are not scored.

Part I

Every road crosses the rhythm

Practice: not scored

The lesson draws the mind’s patterns as three families: a loop of form (1, 2, 4, 8, 7, 5), a rhythm pair (3 and 6), and an axis of equilibrium (9). Its one move is to multiply by 3 and add the digits until one is left (15 becomes 1 + 5 = 6). Tap any number and see where one step takes it.

Tap a number in the loop of form.

Loop numbers tried: 0 of 6

Every first step from the loop lands on 3 or 6. With this one move, the loop does not reach 9 in a single step. It goes through the rhythm pair first. That is the lesson’s picture of why rhythm is the fastest path to coherence. (§2)

Part V

The candle in the still room

Practice: not scored

Holy Mother gave this image of what happens to a restless mind that keeps the Name. Here is the flame, and the wind. Each press of the button is one repetition of the Name. There is no target and no count to reach.

Repetitions: 0

Two things Holy Mother adds

On not waiting for the mind to be calm: Repeat the Name of God, whether your mind is concentrated or not. (§3)

On sincerity over quantity: Repeating the Name of God once, with the mind controlled, is equivalent to a million repetitions with the mind away from God. (§3)

The lesson’s point is that the practice does not wait for a calm mind, and that presence, not the count, carries it.

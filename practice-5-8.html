Part II

Two registers of śaktipāta

Practice: not scored

The lesson says The recognition was not his accomplishment. It was given. (§2.3) It names the same fact at two registers.

The mind-wave equation register

At the mind-wave equation register, śaktipāta is entrainment.

In temporal-domain language, the teacher’s presence raised the signal-to-noise ratio of Manmatha Nath’s system far above what his own practice could have produced in that hour.

The Pratyabhijñā register

Śaktipāta at this register is not a transmission between two consciousnesses but a self-recognition by the one consciousness across the apparent gap.

The lesson says These are not two competing accounts. They are the same fact described at two registers.

Groove and Loop: Saṁskāra or Vāsanā?

The Mind in the Light of Modern and Spiritual Sciences · Advanced

Lesson 4.5 describes a saṁskāra as a self-sustaining loop that a matching cue wakes and that each firing deepens, and a vāsanā as related loops coupled into a larger pattern. Here you will wake, deepen and couple loops on a simple drawing, and then sort eight short cards by scale.

Readout:

The lesson’s description of a loop

Self-sustenance. A torus, once energised, sustains its own pulsation over time.

Triggered firing. The cue does not create the saṁskāra; it wakes it.

Reinforcement. Every activation of a saṁskāra adds energy to the loop, deepening the groove.

Non-interference. Multiple toroidal loops can coexist in the same field at different scales and orientations without cancelling each other out.

Aggregation. Smaller toroidal loops with overlapping frequencies can couple into a larger toroidal structure. This is the mechanism behind vāsanā.

(§3)

Saṁskāras are not the enemy

The lesson is not “saṁskāras are bad; eliminate them.” The lesson is structural: saṁskāras are what we are, at the level of manifest mental life. The structural content of the filter is the totality of one’s saṁskāras. (§3)

Part II

Saṁskāra or vāsanā?

Scored: first pick counts

The lesson separates the two by scale: Where a saṁskāra fires in a specific situation, a vāsanā shapes how all situations of a whole kind are received. (§3) Read each card by its scale. Whether the pattern is welcome or not does not decide it. The people are made up.
